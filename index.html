Official @ThroneEscrow Channel.

Operated by reputable providers, our service guarantees secure and professional transaction handling so you can trade with confidence.

Fees are set at 1.5% of the transaction amount, with a minimum fee of $2. During the initial launch period, the service will operate at 0% fee.

Allowed Transactions

  • Social media usernames and accounts
  • Gaming accounts
  • Game keys or digital licenses
  • Similar digital assets agreed by both parties

Prohibited Transactions

  • Real-world or physical items
  • Illegal goods or services
  • Malicious software, exploits, stolen assets or fraud-related items
  • Anything violating applicable laws or platform rules

Any request involving prohibited items will be refused immediately.
