Operated by reputable providers, our service guarantees secure and professional transaction handling so you can trade with confidence every step of the way.

Allowed Transactions

Royal Escrow only provides middleman services for digital goods, including but not limited to:

  • Social media usernames and accounts
  • Gaming accounts
  • Game keys or other digital licenses
  • Similar digital assets agreed upon by both parties

Prohibited Transactions

We do not middleman any transactions involving:

  • Real-world/physical items
  • Illegal goods or services
  • Malicious software, exploits, stolen assets, or fraud-related items
  • Anything that violates applicable laws or platform rules

Fees
